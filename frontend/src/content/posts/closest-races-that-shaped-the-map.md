## Margins tell a different story
Final seat totals can make an election look straightforward, but close races reveal where the political map was actually fragile.

## What to watch in a close-race table
- vote margin in absolute votes
- margin as a share of votes cast
- turnout context when the electorate size is known
- whether the winner also represented a wider province trend

## Why close races matter
Constituencies decided by a small margin often become the first places to revisit when parties plan their next campaign. They also help explain why the national result can feel more volatile than the final seat count suggests.
