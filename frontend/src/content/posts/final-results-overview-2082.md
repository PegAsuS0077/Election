## The national picture
The 2082 House of Representatives result is no longer a moving election-night story. It is now a settled national dataset that lets us compare provinces, measure competitiveness, and understand which parties converted vote strength into seats.

## What matters in the final archive
- final declared constituency winners
- overall party seat totals across FPTP and PR
- province-by-province seat concentration
- the gap between raw votes and actual seats won

## Why this archive matters
A final-results archive is more useful than a countdown dashboard once polling is over. Readers now want context: which provinces were decisive, which constituencies were close, and which candidates overperformed.

Start with the Analysis page for margins and turnout, then use the Parties and Constituency pages to drill into local outcomes. The News section will continue to publish explainers built on the same archived dataset.
