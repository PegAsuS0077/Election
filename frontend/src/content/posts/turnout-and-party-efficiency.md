## Turnout is only the first layer
Participation tells us where voters showed up in force, but turnout alone does not decide representation. The more interesting question is which parties converted actual votes into seats.

## Two useful comparisons
- turnout by province and constituency
- constituency vote share versus total seats won

## Reading efficiency carefully
Seat efficiency is not a moral score. It simply shows how concentrated or well-distributed a party's support was under the electoral system used for this election.
